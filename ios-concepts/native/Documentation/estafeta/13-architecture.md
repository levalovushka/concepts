# Architecture and module seams

- **Product Blueprint module:** entities, actions, invariants, navigation and states.
- **Native Shell Compiler module:** auth, session, theme, Liquid Glass TabView, Lucide product chrome and capture instrumentation.
- **Capability Runtime module:** real iOS requests, platform operation and persisted product outcome.
- **Product UI module:** SwiftUI screen bodies and product reducers only.
- **Documentation Compiler module:** this reproducible handoff kit.
- **Evidence module:** XCUI journeys, screenshots, geometry and visual review receipts.

The app-specific renderer may not redeclare shell or platform infrastructure.
