# Visual Direction Contract

Visual design starts only after a verified Product Contract, selected World
Model and Experience Contract. It cannot invent screens, actions, entities,
permissions or product copy meaning.

## Three directions, two agents, one receipt

`developVisualDirection({ factoryArtifact, experienceContract, generator,
evaluator })` asks the generator for exactly three materially different
directions. A separate evaluator scores every direction on:

- product hierarchy;
- native coherence;
- cross-screen consistency;
- state completeness;
- strategy integrity;
- visual risk.

Every axis must clear 8.5. The generator cannot score itself. Selection retains
all candidates, independent scores and rejection reasons in a stable receipt.

## Calibration without template copying

`native/VisualCalibrations/catalog.json` separates reusable visual grammar from
product content:

- `looks-vk-mimicry-v1` is the approved VK iOS golden concept. Only flat VK
  chrome, density, semantic roles, attached social actions and icon policy are
  transferable. Looks entities, navigation and feature names are forbidden.
- `native-product-baseline-v1` is a system baseline for differentiation, not a
  falsely promoted concept. `today` and `nakat` are explicitly excluded as
  positive exemplars.

VK Music, VK Video and OK mimicry cannot reuse the VK calibration. Each remains
blocked until its own reference profile and evidence package are ready.

## Renderer obligations

Every direction owns semantic tokens, icon policy, screen families and one
component recipe per Experience Contract screen. A recipe names a supported
native primitive, anatomy, applicable states and prohibitions.

The release renderer must return:

- exact Product, Experience and Visual Contract identities;
- the exact selected tokens and icon policy;
- `consumedRecipeRoles` covering every selected recipe;
- one fresh capture for every applicable screen/state.

Unknown or ignored recipes, token drift, icon drift and incomplete captures
block review before a score is requested.

## Visual critic

The production critic uses a dedicated visual-model operation. A text-only
structured generator is rejected. It must inspect every PNG and score ten axes:
product identity, task clarity, information hierarchy, composition density,
cross-screen consistency, visual craft, interaction predictability, state
quality, accessibility and strategy integrity.

One axis below 8.5 blocks delivery. At most two revisions follow the first
render; the third failed review ends the run with addressable diagnostics.
